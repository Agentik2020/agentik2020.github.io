About the game

Full description available in English.

👻 Untangle arrow puzzles and watch entire pictures disappear, one clever move at a time!

🧩 Welcome to Arrows: Ghost Puzzle, a relaxing logic game with a twist. Every level is a picture woven from winding arrows. Tap an arrow and the entire line slides in the direction of its arrowhead, even around bends. But if another arrow blocks the way, it crashes and costs you one of your three lives for that level. Find the right order and clear the whole picture to win!

✨ Discover the Ghost Phase. As you progress, arrows appear in three colors, and one color becomes a dashed ghost. Other arrows can pass straight through ghost lines — and a ghost arrow can pass through solid lines too. The ghost color rotates after each move, so choosing when to move is just as important as choosing which arrow to remove. The indicator below the board shows the current phase.

🐱 Solve pictures, not just grids. Arrows form cats, hearts, rockets, castles, animals and many other recognizable silhouettes. Levels are generated with a solution, and the puzzles grow larger and denser as you advance. Every fifth level brings a bigger boss challenge. Zoom and move around the board to inspect crowded shapes.

⚡ Keep up a streak of quick successful moves to build combos and score more points. When a puzzle seems impossible, use four boosters: Hint shows a movable arrow, Eraser removes a chosen arrow, Paths reveals which routes are safe or blocked, and Phase Shift changes the ghost color without spending a move. The game introduces its mechanics step by step.

🎁 Earn coins and stars for clearing levels, collect daily rewards and discover occasional gems. Spend rewards on helpful boosters, or return to try another challenge. With an expanding level map, there's always another picture to untangle.

🌙 Play at your own pace with no level timer. Switch between light and dark themes, enjoy sound and music, and play the core puzzles offline. Pinch to zoom, pan around large boards, and double-tap to fit everything on screen. The interface supports 31 languages.

Arrows: Ghost Puzzle is in development. The game includes an economy designed around optional advertising and in-app purchases; some store or advertising features may depend on release and platform availability.
